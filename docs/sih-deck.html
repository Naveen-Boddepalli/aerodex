01

Proposed Solution

AeroDex  ·  PS SIH26056  ·  MoSPI
THE MEASUREMENT GAP

CPI carries one airfare figure a month. Airline pricing moves several times a day, and moves differently by route, by carrier and by how far ahead a seat is booked. Neither the size nor the shape of that movement is measured anywhere today.

THE INSTRUMENT WE PROPOSE

A 60-route panel weighted by DGCA passenger traffic, observed at 7 booking horizons across 3 fixed IST slots — 420 strata, roughly 1,155 stratum-slots a day. Jevons elementary index, hedonic quality adjustment, Lowe aggregation, chain-linked and seasonally adjusted.

PROVEN

Reproducibility is a property, not a promise

compute_index() is a pure function — no clock, no database, no network. A test parses the module's own source to prove it. Frozen panel in, frozen hash out.

ENFORCED

Compliance is executable code

No authentication, no CAPTCHA, no personal data, a 20-second floor per host, robots.txt honoured at run time. We wrote our own RFC 9309 parser after finding four defects in Python's.

PUBLISHED

It discloses its own limits

Every index point carries its coverage ratio, imputed weight share and config hash. The publisher refuses to release a run it cannot stand behind — refusal is code, not convention.

ZERO COST

Costed to be handed over

Permanent free tiers only: no trials, no credits, no vendor lock-in. The system transfers to a ministry with no invoice attached and no licence conversation.

CORE FINDING

The finding that shapes this solution

Four research passes over 40+ candidate sources, each source resolved on nine independent axes, produced one structural result: every source that carries a booking horizon prohibits automated collection, and every source that permits automated collection carries no booking horizon. That is look-to-book economics, not coincidence — distribution meters search and recovers the cost from bookings, and an index books nothing. So the blocker is statutory authority, and the sponsor of this problem statement already holds it.

EVERY LAYER CHOSEN AGAINST A ₹0-FOREVER CONSTRAINT
Compute

Oracle Always Free A1, Indian region — Actions cron drifts 10–30 min, Render sleeps, Fly.io is credits. An Indian egress IP matters: fare pages price by geography.

Database

PostgreSQL 16 + TimescaleDB, self-hosted — 2 GB/year kills Supabase (500 MB, pauses after 7 idle days) and Neon (0.5 GB).

Queue

Postgres FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED — Celery and Redis cost a second daemon and ~200 MB of RAM for 1,155 tasks a day.

Scheduler

systemd timers, not cron — OnCalendar, jitter control and journal integration, at no cost and no extra process.

Publication

Static artifacts on Cloudflare R2 and Pages — the dashboard keeps serving when the free VM does not.

Monitoring

Grafana Cloud Free, UptimeRobot, Telegram — off-box on purpose: self-hosted monitoring dies exactly when it is needed.

WHY IT HOLDS

Look-to-book economics

Every distribution channel meters search and recovers the cost from bookings. An index books nothing. Duffel writes it into contract; Travelpayouts states it in plain words. This is why further source-searching has negative expected value — we stopped.

TESTED, THEN KILLED

The last escape route

Rule 135 tariff sheets looked free, structured and archived. We parsed 14 of them: a fare level is a band, not a price (median max/min 1.25–1.52×, p90 2.82×), levels move −21% to +10% month on month, and the route panel churns to 46.6% survival in a single step.

THE PATH FORWARD

Authority, not engineering

MoSPI already collects airfares from online platforms under the Collection of Statistics Act 2008 (CPI 2024 FAQ, Q27), and DGCA has already supplied airfare data to MoSPI — minuted as recommendation 11(a). Two letters are drafted and ready to send.

RECORDED IN ADVANCE, NOT IMPROVISED — if access does not land inside the SIH timeline, the decision moves to redesigning the index around whatever bounded data the permission path does yield; that revisit condition is written into the decision record. Meanwhile nothing else is blocked — the engine, the hedonic model, the validation path and the eSankhyiki benchmark are all buildable today, and largely built.
04

Impact and Benefits

Who gains, and what they gain that does not exist today
MoSPI

A sub-monthly airfare signal

CPI publishes one airfare index a month. AeroDex produces a daily national series with route, carrier and booking-horizon breakdowns beneath it — granularity an item index cannot carry. Imputation share and coverage ratio ship with every point, so the Ministry can see how much of each number was actually observed.

DGCA

An evidence base for tariff monitoring

The regulator monitors fares on selected routes today. A continuously observed, horizon-controlled panel turns spot checks into a series: which routes move, how far ahead the movement starts, and whether a festival window or a capacity change is driving it.

TRAVELLERS

Fare behaviour made legible

Published openly, methodology alongside: how far ahead to book on a given route, what a festival window costs, and how last-minute pricing differs between full-service and low-cost carriers. The calendar already encodes 8 festivals and 3 vacation windows with lead-day markers.

THE WIDER SYSTEM

Reusable well beyond airfares

The reproducibility machinery — append-only archive, hashed config, a pure index function, golden tests — is not airfare-specific. Any high-frequency price collection the statistical system takes on can reuse it. At ₹0 recurring, handover carries no budget line.

INDEPENDENTLY CORROBORATED — MoSPI's own CPI 2024 series uses Jevons for elementary indices and a Laspeyres-family aggregation above it — the two most consequential choices AeroDex made, arrived at independently and before we knew. The IMF Technical Assistance mission to MoSPI separately recommended a greater variety of advance-purchase timings and treating the airline as a price-determining characteristic; the 7-horizon, carrier-attributed panel already does both.
