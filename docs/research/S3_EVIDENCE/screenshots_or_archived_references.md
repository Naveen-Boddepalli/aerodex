weeks before `plan.md` was written, and Amadeus decommissioned an entire developer portal
inside this project's lifetime. A URL plus a date is weaker evidence than a capture.

**Before any of these figures appears in a public submission**, archive the four pricing
pages and the Duffel Services Agreement:


Record the resulting timestamped URLs in this file. Until then, treat every price in
`S3_PRICING_AND_COST_MODEL.md` as **read on 25 August 2026 and not independently archived** —
which is exactly the standard the previous S3 applied to the DGCA direction of 13 May 2025,
and it should not be relaxed now that the conclusions are favourable.
